# Executive Case Study: End-to-End Retail Revenue & Profit Margin Optimization

Language: **SQL (PostgreSQL)** | Database Management | Visualizations: **Power BI**

## 📂 1. Strategic Project Overview
An executive-level analysis of enterprise retail sales data utilizing **PostgreSQL** for querying transactional data and **Power BI** for visual reporting to evaluate revenue health, uncover profitability trends, and identify operational margin leaks.

## ❓ 2. Business Questions
* **Financial Performance:** Overall sales, profit margins, and the impact of promotional discounts on net profitability.
* **Geographic Analysis:** Top-performing markets versus regions running at a loss.
* **Product Analysis:** Volume drivers versus actual profit, identifying high-volume products leaking cash.
* **Customer & Operations:** Valuable customer segments and cost-effective shipping methods.

## 🛠️ 3. Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Cleaning & Exploration:** Microsoft Excel
* **Database Management & Analysis:** SQL (PostgreSQL), pgAdmin
* **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Microsoft Power BI

---

## 🔍 4. Analysis, Findings & Insights
* **Overall Financial Performance:** Total sales of ~$2.3M, yielding $287k profit (12.45% margin).
* **Discount Impact:** Low discounts (1-20%) are manageable, mid-tier (21-40%) reduce average order value, and discounts above 40% result in net losses.
* **Product Profitability:** **Technology** drives net profit, while **Furniture** (specifically *Tables* and *Bookcases*) suffers structural losses.
* **Regional & Customer Segmentation:** The **West** region shows highest conversion; top 10 customers drive significant revenue.
